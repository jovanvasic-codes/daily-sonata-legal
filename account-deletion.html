Delete your account

Daily Sonata

You can delete your Daily Sonata account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Daily Sonata.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and the app returns to anonymous use.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your account record and email from our authentication provider (Supabase). Your subscription is managed by Google Play; cancel it from your Google Play account if you no longer want it. Saved pieces, collections, and preferences are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

If you cannot access the app

Email itdjordjegligorijevic@gmail.com from the address tied to your account and ask us to delete it. We will process the request within 30 days.
