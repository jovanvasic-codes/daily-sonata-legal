Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Daily Sonata ("the app", "we", "us") is a daily classical music discovery app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum the app needs, and we do not sell your data.

Using the app without an account

You can use Daily Sonata without creating an account. In that case the app uses an anonymous identifier so the service works and so we can measure how features are used. Your saved pieces, collections, and preferences are stored on your device, not on our servers.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account, we process your email address and authentication details through our infrastructure provider (Supabase) so you can sign in and keep your subscription across devices. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile identifier from Google to create the account. We do not receive your Google password.

What we collect and why

  • Account data (only if you create one): email address and an account identifier, to sign you in and restore purchases. Provider: Supabase.
  • Anonymous identifier: a random id used to operate the service and to tie analytics events together.
  • Product analytics: in-app events such as app opens, screens viewed, pieces played, discoveries, onboarding steps, and purchase events, plus basic device and app version info. We use this to understand what works and improve the app. Provider: PostHog.
  • Crash and error diagnostics: crash reports, error details, and device or OS information, so we can fix problems. Provider: Sentry.
  • Purchases: your subscription status and purchase identifiers, to unlock premium features and restore them. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not by us. Providers: Google Play and RevenueCat.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or microphone, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

Stored on your device

Saved pieces, collections, listening streak, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes this local data.

Third parties and audio

Short audio previews and streaming links are provided by third-party music services (such as Deezer, iTunes, and Apple or Spotify search links). Opening a link hands you off to that service under its own terms and privacy policy.

Data retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Analytics and diagnostics are retained only as long as needed to run and improve the service. When you delete your account, your account record is removed from our authentication provider.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. See our account deletion page.
  • Access or request removal of your data by emailing us.

Children

Daily Sonata is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
